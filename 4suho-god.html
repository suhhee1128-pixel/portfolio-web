4suho god

An interactive mixed-media exhibition that transformed a physical space into a participatory narrative through sculptural installations, myth-inspired characters, and on-site AR interactions.
The project integrated physical sculptures with Instagram-based AR filters and hands-on engagement points, encouraging visitors to actively explore the exhibition rather than passively observe.
Planned and executed the full exhibition experience, from spatial layout and visual identity to digital interaction design and on-site touchpoints.
The exhibition attracted 10,000+ visitors, generating high on-site engagement and widespread social sharing across campus and social media channels.
Tools used include Blender for 3D form development and Figma for experience flow and visual planning.
