ECLIPSE CHASER

Kookmin University Spatial Design Gallery
Mar 2022 – Sep 2022

I led the end-to-end planning and experience strategy for ECLIPSE CHASER, translating the abstract concept of a total solar eclipse into a cohesive spatial and emotional exhibition experience.
Using the metaphor of eclipse and persona, I defined the exhibition's core vision and experience architecture, shaping spatial flow, visual identity, and on-site interactions into a unified narrative.
The exhibition delivered a consistent, user-centered experience to 7,000+ visitors, demonstrating my ability to scale abstract concepts into tangible spatial experiences through cross-functional collaboration.
